I am an assistant professor in [the Division of AI Data Convergence](https://aidata.hufs.ac.kr/) at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies ([HUFS](https://www.hufs.ac.kr/)), and I lead the [Data Intelligence and Applications Lab](https://dia.hufs.ac.kr/). I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from Sungkyunkwan University ([SKKU](https://www.skku.edu/)) in 2026, where I was a member of the Data Intelligence and Learning Lab ([DIAL Lab](https://dial.skku.edu/)) advised by [Prof. Jongwuk Lee](https://jongwuklee.weebly.com/).

My research spans diverse recommendation tasks, including traditional collaborative filtering, sequential and session-based recommendation, conversational recommendation, and generative recommendation. Recently, I have been exploring the integration of large language models and multimodal data with recommender systems.
